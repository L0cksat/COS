# COS — Product Vision

## 1. Overview

COS (Career Operating System) is a personal career management platform designed to help individuals understand their current professional position, define meaningful career goals, and create actionable roadmaps to achieve them.

COS treats career development as a journey rather than simply a collection of job applications.

---

## 2. Problem

Career development is frequently fragmented across multiple platforms, applications, documents, and personal notes.

Users may manage their:

- CV
- LinkedIn profile
- Job applications
- Skills
- Learning
- Projects
- Interviews
- Networking
- Career goals

across completely separate systems.

This fragmentation makes it difficult to maintain a coherent view of the overall career journey.

COS aims to provide a structured system that connects these elements.

---

## 3. Core Question

COS is built around three fundamental questions:

### Where am I?

Understand the user's current career position, skills, experience, projects, qualifications, and professional activity.

### Where do I want to go?

Define career goals, target roles, desired skills, milestones, and long-term objectives.

### What should I do next?

Convert the gap between the current state and desired state into actionable tasks, learning objectives, projects, applications, and milestones.

---

## 4. Product Philosophy

COS is based on four principles:

### Structure

Convert broad career ambitions into structured goals, roadmaps, milestones, and tasks.

### Visibility

Give users a clear view of their current position and progress.

### Context

Connect career goals, skills, learning, projects, applications, and professional development.

### Intelligence

Use AI to analyse structured career information and provide contextual recommendations.

---

## 5. AI Philosophy

COS is not intended to be a generic LLM interface or chatbot wrapper.

The product should be built around structured career information first, with AI operating as an intelligence layer over that information.

AI should:

- Analyse career context
- Identify potential skills gaps
- Highlight patterns
- Suggest actions
- Assist with planning
- Provide contextual recommendations

AI should not:

- Make career decisions on behalf of users
- Replace professional judgement
- Generate recommendations without relevant context
- Become the primary purpose of the application

The guiding principle is:

> **Structured data first. AI second.**

---

## 6. Target Users

COS initially targets four primary user groups:

### Students

People beginning to explore professional careers.

### Graduates

People transitioning from education into employment.

### Career Changers

Professionals transitioning into a different career or industry.

### Established Professionals

People seeking progression, development, or a new professional direction.

---

## 7. What COS Is Not

COS is not:

- A recruitment agency
- A job board
- A replacement for LinkedIn
- A generic AI chatbot
- A generic project-management application
- A standalone CV generator
- A learning platform
- An automated career decision-maker

COS is a personal career management and planning system.

---

## 8. Core Product Loop

COS is based around the following lifecycle:

Current State
→ Career Goal
→ Skills / Experience Gap
→ Roadmap
→ Actions
→ Progress
→ Updated Current State

This creates a continuous career-development loop.

---

## 9. MVP Direction

The initial MVP will focus on the fundamental career-planning workflow.

The MVP should allow a user to:

1. Create a career profile.
2. Define a career goal.
3. Create a roadmap toward that goal.
4. Define milestones.
5. Create actionable tasks.
6. Track progress.
7. Review their current career position.

Additional functionality such as job tracking, skills management, AI analysis, and external integrations will be considered after the core workflow has been validated.

---

## 10. COS Ecosystem

COS may eventually evolve into a broader ecosystem of structured personal operating systems.

### Career Operating System

Manage professional journeys.

### Writer Operating System

Manage creative writing journeys, including:

- Characters
- Relationships
- Plot
- Chapters
- Scenes
- Timelines
- Locations
- Worldbuilding
- Story threads
- Continuity

Writer OS is a future product concept and is outside the initial COS MVP.

Both products share the same philosophy:

> **Structure first. Intelligence second.**

---

## 11. Product North Star

COS exists to help people answer:

> **Where am I, where do I want to go, and what should I do next?**

Turn uncertainty into structure. Turn structure into action. Turn action into measurable progress.
