# COS — User Personas

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the primary user personas for COS (Career Operating System).

Personas are used to represent the different types of users COS is intended to serve and to provide a reference point when evaluating product requirements and features.

COS is intended to support people at different stages of their professional journeys. While their circumstances may differ, they share a common need:

> **To understand their current position, define where they want to go, and determine what they should do next.**

---

# 2. Persona Overview

| Persona | Primary Situation | Core Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Student | Preparing to enter the professional world | "I don't know where to start." |
| Graduate | Transitioning from education into employment | "I've finished studying. How do I get hired?" |
| Career Changer | Moving from one career to another | "How do I get from where I am to where I want to be?" |
| Established Professional | Seeking progression or a new direction | "How do I get to the next stage?" |
| Developer / Research Persona | Real-world product discovery case | "How do I organise and optimise my own career transition?" |

The first four personas represent the intended product audience.

The Developer / Research Persona exists specifically to support product discovery and validation during COS development. It represents the type of real-world career journey being used to test COS's assumptions and workflows.

---

# 3. Persona 1 — Student

## Profile

A student who is beginning to think seriously about their professional future but may not yet have a clearly defined career direction.

They may be studying at university, vocational training, college, or another form of formal education.

## Primary Goal

Develop an understanding of potential career directions and create a plan for entering the professional world.

## Typical Questions

- What careers could I pursue?
- What skills do I need?
- What should I learn?
- What projects should I build?
- What experience do employers expect?
- How should I prepare for my first job?

## Goals

- Explore career possibilities
- Identify relevant skills
- Define an initial career direction
- Build experience
- Create projects
- Develop a professional portfolio
- Prepare for employment

## Frustrations

- Lack of professional experience
- Uncertainty about career direction
- Difficulty understanding employer expectations
- Information spread across many platforms
- Difficulty determining which skills to prioritise

## COS Needs

COS should help students:

- Define potential career goals
- Identify required skills
- Create learning roadmaps
- Track projects
- Record achievements
- Define milestones
- Prepare for entering the job market

## Example COS Journey

```text
Explore Careers
      ↓
Select Target
      ↓
Identify Required Skills
      ↓
Create Learning Roadmap
      ↓
Build Projects
      ↓
Develop Portfolio
      ↓
Begin Job Search
```

# 4. Persona 2 — Graduate

## Profile

A recently graduated student transitioning from education into professional employment.

This user may have qualifications and theoretical knowledge but limited professional experience.

## Primary Goal

Secure their first relevant professional role and establish a career trajectory.

## Typical Questions

- Am I ready for the job market?
- Which roles should I apply for?
- What skills am I missing?
- Is my CV effective?
- Is my portfolio strong enough?
- Why am I not getting interviews?
- What should I improve next?

## Goals

- Obtain relevant employment
- Build professional experience
- Improve employability
- Develop a strong portfolio
- Build professional networks
- Prepare for interviews
- Identify and close skills gaps

## Frustrations

- Limited professional experience
- Large number of competing candidates
- Uncertainty about application strategy
- Difficulty measuring job-search progress
- Difficulty identifying why applications are unsuccessful
- Lack of a structured career-development process

## COS Needs

COS should help graduates:

- Define target roles
- Assess their current position
- Identify skills gaps
- Create development plans
- Manage applications
- Track interviews
- Track portfolio projects
- Record professional achievements
- Monitor progress

## Example COS Journey

```text
Graduation
    ↓
Career Goal
    ↓
Skills Assessment
    ↓
Skills Gap
    ↓
Portfolio Development
    ↓
Job Search
    ↓
Applications
    ↓
Interviews
    ↓
Employment
    ↓
Professional Development
```

# 5. Persona 3 — Career Changer

## Profile

An established professional who wants to transition into a different career, industry, or professional discipline.

The user may already possess significant experience but may not know how that experience translates into their target career.

## Primary Goal

Create a realistic transition from their current professional position to a new career.

## Typical Questions

- Which of my existing skills are transferable?
- What skills am I missing?
- What qualifications do I need?
- How long could the transition take?
- What projects or experience should I build?
- How can I demonstrate that I am capable of working in the new field?
- How should I position my previous experience?

## Goals

- Define a realistic target career
- Identify transferable skills
- Identify skills gaps
- Develop new competencies
- Build relevant experience
- Reposition professional experience
- Enter the target industry

## Frustrations

- Uncertainty about transferable skills
- Difficulty determining what qualifications are actually necessary
- Lack of relevant professional experience
- Difficulty explaining the career transition
- Financial and time constraints
- Managing the existing career while preparing for the next one

## COS Needs

COS should help career changers:

- Map their existing skills
- Define target roles
- Identify gaps
- Build transition roadmaps
- Track learning
- Track portfolio projects
- Manage applications
- Document transferable experience
- Measure progress toward the target career

## Example COS Journey

```text
Current Career
      ↓
Target Career
      ↓
Transferable Skills
      ↓
Skills Gap
      ↓
Learning Plan
      ↓
Relevant Experience
      ↓
Portfolio
      ↓
Applications
      ↓
Career Transition
```

# 6. Persona 4 — Established Professional

## Profile

A professional with established experience who wants to progress, specialise, change direction, or pursue a new professional objective.

Unlike the Graduate or Career Changer, this user may already have significant professional credibility and experience.

## Primary Goal

Plan and manage the next stage of their professional development.

## Typical Questions

- What should my next career milestone be?
- What skills do I need to progress?
- Should I specialise or move into management?
- What qualifications would help?
- How can I demonstrate readiness for promotion?
- Am I progressing toward my long-term goals?
- Should I consider a different company or industry?

## Goals

- Career progression
- Professional development
- Specialisation
- Promotion
- Increased responsibility
- Networking
- Long-term career planning

## Frustrations

- Lack of visibility into long-term career progression
- Unclear development priorities
- Difficulty measuring professional growth
- Career stagnation
- Lack of structured long-term planning

## COS Needs

COS should help professionals:

- Define long-term objectives
- Create career roadmaps
- Track competencies
- Track achievements
- Plan professional development
- Record projects and experience
- Monitor progress toward milestones
- Evaluate potential career directions

## Example COS Journey

```text
Current Role
      ↓
Career Objective
      ↓
Required Competencies
      ↓
Development Plan
      ↓
Professional Milestones
      ↓
Progress
      ↓
Next Career Stage
```

# 7. Persona 5 — Developer / Research Persona

## Purpose

The Developer / Research Persona is not a separate target market.

It is a product-development persona based on a real-world career transition being used to validate COS's assumptions.

The purpose is to identify genuine problems that COS should solve rather than designing features around hypothetical requirements.

## Profile

A recent vocational graduate transitioning into a junior web development career while simultaneously developing a professional portfolio, improving technical skills, networking, and exploring multiple employment pathways.

The individual may need to manage several parallel career activities at the same time.

## Current Career Activities

Potential activities include:

- Job applications
- CV development
- LinkedIn optimisation
- Portfolio development
- GitHub development
- Technical skills development
- Interview preparation
- Networking
- Professional referrals
- Freelance exploration
- Self-employment exploration
- Personal projects

## Primary Goal

Secure a junior web development position while continuing to develop professional skills and maintaining alternative career pathways.

## Key Questions

- Which roles should I target?
- Is my CV effective?
- Is my LinkedIn profile effective?
- Which projects should I highlight?
- Which technical skills should I improve?
- Which skills are most valuable for my target roles?
- How should I prioritise job applications?
- Why are some applications progressing while others do not?
- How should I prepare for interviews?
- Should I pursue employment, freelance work, self-employment, or multiple pathways?
- What should I work on next?

## Potential Frustrations

- Managing multiple career activities simultaneously
- Difficulty prioritising tasks
- Uncertainty about which skills to improve
- Fragmentation between CV, LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio, and job applications
- Difficulty measuring career progress
- Maintaining multiple potential career pathways
- Limited time available for career development

## COS Opportunity

This persona demonstrates the potential value of bringing multiple career-development activities into a single structured system.

A potential COS representation might look like:

```text
Career Goal
    │
    ├── Target Roles
    │
    ├── Skills
    │    ├── Current Skills
    │    └── Skills Gaps
    │
    ├── Portfolio
    │    └── Projects
    │
    ├── Job Search
    │    ├── Opportunities
    │    ├── Applications
    │    └── Interviews
    │
    ├── Networking
    │
    ├── Learning
    │
    └── Alternative Career Paths
         ├── Freelance
         └── Self-Employment
```

# 8. Shared User Needs

Although the personas have different circumstances, they share several fundamental needs.

## Direction

Users need to understand where they want to go.

## Assessment

Users need to understand their current position.

## Gap Identification

Users need to understand what separates their current position from their desired position.

## Planning

Users need to convert their goals into achievable steps.

## Action

Users need to know what they should work on next.

## Tracking

Users need to see whether they are making progress.

## Adaptation

Users need to adjust their plans as their circumstances change.

# 10. Jobs to Be Done

COS should ultimately help users accomplish the following jobs:

## Job 1 — Define Direction

	When I am uncertain about my professional future, I want to define a meaningful career direction so that I know what I am working toward.
	
## Job 2 — Understand My Position

	When I have a career goal, I want to understand my current skills, experience, and qualifications so that I can determine how prepared I am.
	
## Job 3 — Identify Gaps

	When I know where I want to go, I want to identify the skills and experience I am missing so that I can focus my development efforts.

## Job 4 — Create a Roadmap

	When I understand my gaps, I want to create an actionable roadmap so that I know how to progress toward my goal.

## Job 5 — Prioritise Actions

	When I have many possible career-development activities, I want to know which actions should receive priority so that I can make effective use of my time.

## Job 6 — Track Progress

	When I am working toward a career goal, I want to track my progress so that I can see whether my actions are producing meaningful development.

## Job 7 — Adapt

	When my circumstances or career goals change, I want to update my roadmap so that my career plan remains relevant.

# 11. Persona Design Principle

Personas should be treated as evolving product-development tools rather than fixed descriptions of users.

As COS development progresses, assumptions about these personas should be validated through:

- User interviews
- Usability testing
- Product analytics
- Feedback
- Prototype testing
- Real-world usage

The personas should therefore be updated when evidence demonstrates that user behaviour or requirements differ from the initial assumptions.

---

# 12. Product Discovery Principle

COS development should avoid designing features solely because they appear technically interesting.

Each major feature should ultimately be connected to:

1. A user problem
2. A user need
3. A measurable outcome

The fundamental question should remain:

	Does this help the user understand where they are, where they want to go, or what they should do next?
